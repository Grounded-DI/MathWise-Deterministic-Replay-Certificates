# RH-001H moving-endpoint obstruction

Source: https://github.com/Grounded-DI/Grounded-DI-Riemann_Hypothesis_Research_Moving-Endpoint_Obstruction_to_Global_Second-Tail_Positivity/tree/6da7b21c572d8e382a32c65866fa225e0d558182

The October 4, 2026 record gives an analytic existence argument for arbitrarily large real heights at which the actual-theta second tail is negative at a constructed moving endpoint. It preserves the preceding positive cone in a different endpoint region.

Classification: bounded analytic obstruction theorem with exact finite witness. It does not prove or disprove RH, locate an off-critical-line zero, or establish a negative aggregate transform.
